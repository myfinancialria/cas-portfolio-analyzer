Where do I get my CAS? · What's supported?
  • Get your CAS: CAMS → Statements → CAS or KFintech → CAS. Choose the Detailed statement, since the first investment date (e.g. from 01-Jan-1990) — that unlocks XIRR and the LTCG/STCG estimate.
  • Supported: CAMS/KFintech mail-back CAS, detailed or summary. Not yet: NSDL/CDSL depository e-CAS (different layout). For odd variants, convert with casparser (casparser yourfile.pdf -o out.json) and drop the JSON here — same analysis, guaranteed parse.
  • Parser didn't work? and open a GitHub issue with the (redacted) layout.
